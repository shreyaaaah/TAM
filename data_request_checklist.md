# Data request and first-meeting checklist

What I would ask a client before committing to a seat count, and why each item matters. Everything in the case study that is marked "assumption" is replaced by one of these.

## 1. Questions for the first meeting

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Is "3 days a week" a mandate, a cap, or an average? | Changes the floor and how holiday weeks behave. |
| How is headcount split across the three offices, and is growth planned? | Sets H for each office. The total floor is 1,200 regardless, but each office needs its own number. |
| Are there days or teams that must be in together (anchor days)? | Decides how much we can control k, the peak factor. |
| What share of roles need a fixed seat (reception, labs, support, special equipment)? | Fixed seats cannot be pooled, so they come off the shared pool. |
| What are the lease terms and flexible-space options per office? | Decides which levers are available and how fast. |
| Who owns the decision to add or give back seats, and what thresholds would they accept? | Lets us agree triggers before data arrives. |
| How are visitors, contractors and new joiners handled? | Not in the case, but they raise the real maximum. |
| What can the current booking or badge system log (bookings, check-ins, failed searches)? | I do not assume what a tool logs. This decides whether the seat-denied rate can be measured at all. |

## 2. Data to collect, per office

| Data | Used for | Minimum history |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily badge or check-in counts | Average attendance, peak attendance, k | 8 to 12 weeks |
| Bookings with timestamps | No-show rate, release timing, booking lead time | 8 to 12 weeks |
| Failed seat searches, waitlist, "no seat" reports | Seat-denied rate (unmet demand) | From day one of the pilot |
| Team and zone mapping | Team co-location rate | Current snapshot |
| Public holiday and leave calendar | Holiday-week demand | Current year |
| Cost per seat: rent, services, flexible space | Cost per attended person-day | Latest billing period |
| Short employee pulse survey | What numbers miss (noise, distance from team) | Monthly |

## 3. If the client cannot answer

- Ask for headcount by site first. HR can give it the same day.
- Commit only to the floor (0.6 x headcount per office) plus a labelled placeholder k.
- Start a pilot with a daily cap per office and log failed searches from the first day.
- Review after a few weeks and replace each placeholder with measured data.
